# Demonstration of Microreactor Experiments (DOME) Description

The VTB DOME models are based on the Demonstration of Microreactor Experiments (DOME) facility, a test bed supported by the Nuclear Reactor Innovation Center (NRIC) that is designed to host a variety of microreactor experiments. DOME includes a built-in shield, consisting primarily of concrete and water structures, which protects the greater facility from heat and radiation [!citep](MPR2025thermal). 

The models presented here include:

- [Mesh](microreactors/dome/mesh.md): the mesh of the DOME shield, including the concrete and water structures, insulation, air gaps, doors, and a simplified reactor core.
- [Thermal model](microreactors/dome/thermal.md): the calculation of temperatures in the shield.
