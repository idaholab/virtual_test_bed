VIRTUAL TEST BED
!style-end!

!row!
!col! small=6 medium=6 large=6

The Virtual Test Bed (VTB) is an [open repository](https://github.com/idaholab/virtual_test_bed) containing a wide variety of [documented models](vtb_pages/manual_indexing.md) supporting nuclear energy applications, primarily based on advanced reactor designs. The VTB is sponsored by the [National Reactor Innovation Center (NRIC)](https://nric.inl.gov/) and supports its mission by facilitating the application of advanced modeling & simulation (M&S) tools toward efforts to accelerate the deployment of innovative reactor concepts. A [variety of tools](vtb_pages/codes.md) are represented in these models, primarily those developed under the [Nuclear Energy Advanced Modeling and Simulation (NEAMS) program](https://neams.inl.gov/). Models employing NEAMS codes are continuously tested and maintained so that they always run with current code versions.
